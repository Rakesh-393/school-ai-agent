# Attendance Policy — Paramita Schools (2025-26)

## Minimum attendance
Every student must maintain a minimum of **75% attendance** across the academic
year to be eligible to sit for the final examinations. Attendance is computed on
working days only; declared holidays are excluded from the denominator.

## Marking
Attendance is marked twice daily — once at the 09:00 assembly and once after the
lunch break at 13:30. A student who arrives after 09:15 is marked **late**. Three
"late" marks in a calendar month are counted as one day absent.

## Applying for leave
Planned leave must be applied for at least **three working days in advance**
through the parent portal, or by a signed letter to the class teacher. Medical
leave of more than two consecutive days requires a doctor's certificate submitted
within one week of the student's return.

## Shortfall
If a student's attendance falls below 75%, the class teacher issues a written
notice to the parents. A shortfall condonation may be granted by the Principal
only on medical grounds, and only up to a maximum of 10 percentage points.

## Long absence
An absence of more than 15 consecutive days without intimation may result in the
student's name being struck off the rolls. Re-admission is at the Principal's
discretion and attracts a re-admission fee.
