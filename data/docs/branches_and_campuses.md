# Paramita Schools — Branches and Campuses

Paramita Schools is a group of **five campuses**, all located in Karimnagar,
Telangana. The group has been in education for close to three decades and offers
three curricula across its campuses: the Telangana State board (SSC), CBSE, and
Cambridge International Education.

Common enquiry number for all campuses: **+91-8886127373 / +91-9885136655**
Group website: paramitaschools.in

## 1. Paramita High School — Mankammathota
State board (TS SSC), Pre-K to Grade 10. Established 18 June 1996; this is the
founding campus of the group. Website: paramitahighschool.in

## 2. Paramita School — Ponnam Complex
State board (TS SSC), Pre-K to Grade 10, at Ponnam Complex, Karimnagar.

## 3. Paramita Heritage School — Padmanagar
CBSE, affiliation number 3630258, Grade 1 to Grade 10. Uses the XSEED curriculum
through Grade 8 and the CBSE syllabus for Grades 9 and 10. Established 2 June
2005. Principal: Parankusham Gopikrishna (M.Sc, B.Ed.).
Address: Vemulawada Road, Beside Walmart, Padmanagar, Karimnagar - 505001.
Website: paramitaheritageschool.in

## 4. Paramita World School — Alugunoor
CBSE, Grade 1 to Grade 10, on the Hyderabad Highway Road at Alugunoor. Also
referred to as IRIS World School. Website: paramitaworldschool.in

## 5. Explorica Premium School — Jyothinagar
Cambridge International Education (CIE). Toddlers, Pre-K and Prep through to
Grade 7. Address: 2-10-1473, Jyothi Nagar, Karimnagar - 505001.
Phone: +91 91609 36655. Email: contact@explorica.in. Website: explorica.in

## Choosing a campus
Admission enquiries for any campus may be made on the common enquiry number, or
in person at the campus front office. Transfers between campuses within the group
are possible at the start of an academic year, subject to seat availability and
to the receiving campus's curriculum — a student moving from a State board campus
to a CBSE or Cambridge campus may be asked to sit a placement assessment.
