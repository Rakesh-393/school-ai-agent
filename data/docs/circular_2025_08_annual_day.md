# Circular 2025/08 — Annual Day and Holiday Schedule

Date: 12 August 2025
To: All parents

## Annual Day
The Annual Day will be held on **Saturday, 13 December 2025** at the school
auditorium from 16:00. Each family is entitled to two passes; additional passes
may be requested through the front office until 30 November, subject to seating.

Rehearsals for participating students begin on 3 November and are held during the
last two periods on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Participation is voluntary and does
not affect attendance records.

## Holiday schedule for the remainder of 2025-26
- Independence Day: 15 August 2025
- Dussehra break: 29 September to 3 October 2025
- Diwali break: 20 October to 25 October 2025
- Christmas break: 24 December 2025 to 1 January 2026
- Republic Day: 26 January 2026
- Holi: 4 March 2026

The school reopens after each break on the next working day. Any change to this
schedule will be communicated by a separate circular at least one week in advance.
