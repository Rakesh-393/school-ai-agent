# Fee Policy and Payment Schedule (2025-26)

## Structure
The annual tuition fee is billed in two terms of Rs 45,000 each. Transport is
billed separately at Rs 8,500 per term and depends on the bus route allotted.
Laboratory and activity charges are included in the tuition fee.

## Due dates
- Term 1: due on or before **15 June 2025**
- Term 2: due on or before **15 November 2025**

## Late payment
A late fee of Rs 500 is charged for each calendar month, or part thereof, that a
payment is delayed. If fees remain unpaid for more than 60 days past the due
date, the student may not be permitted to appear for term examinations until the
balance is cleared.

## Payment methods
Fees may be paid online through the parent portal (UPI, net banking, debit card),
or by cheque or demand draft drawn in favour of "Paramita Schools".
Cash payments above Rs 10,000 are not accepted.

## Concessions
A sibling concession of 10% on tuition is available for the second and each
subsequent child enrolled at the school. Staff wards receive a 50% tuition
concession. Concessions must be applied for at the start of the academic year and
are not applied retrospectively.

## Refunds
Tuition fees are refundable only if withdrawal is intimated in writing before the
term begins. The admission fee and caution deposit are non-refundable.
