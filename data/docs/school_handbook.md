# Parent Handbook — Paramita Schools

## School timings
Classes run from 09:00 to 15:30, Monday to Friday. On the second and fourth
Saturday of each month the school is open from 09:00 to 12:30 for remedial
classes and club activities. Other Saturdays are holidays.

## Uniform
The regular uniform is worn Monday to Thursday. The house uniform with white
canvas shoes is worn on Friday. On Saturdays students may wear the sports
uniform. Long hair must be tied back. Only plain studs are permitted as jewellery.

## Transport
Bus routes are allotted at the start of the year based on the residential address
on record. A change of route may be requested through the transport office with
two weeks' notice, subject to seat availability on the requested route. Students
must be at the pickup point five minutes before the scheduled time; the bus will
not wait beyond two minutes.

## Communication with teachers
Parents may meet subject teachers on the first Saturday of every month between
10:00 and 12:00, by appointment made through the parent portal. Urgent matters
should be routed through the class teacher, not directly to subject teachers.

## Examinations and reporting
There are two unit tests and two term examinations per academic year. Report
cards are published on the parent portal within ten working days of the last
examination. A student must score a minimum of 40% in each subject to be
promoted to the next grade.

## Mobile phones and devices
Mobile phones are not permitted on campus. A phone found in a student's
possession is confiscated and returned only to a parent. Smart watches with
calling or messaging features fall under the same rule.

## Medical
The school nurse is available during school hours. Parents must declare allergies
and chronic conditions at admission and update the school in writing of any
change. The school does not administer prescription medication without a written
request from the parent and a copy of the prescription.
